# Image asset generation

Built-in image generation was used; no external CLI or API key was required. Generated outputs were copied into `docs/assets/` and originals retained.

## MOBA battlefield

Production landscape gameplay-world background for an interactive mobile MOBA touch-testing screen, 16:9. Match the normal isometric gameplay-world visual vocabulary of Honor of Kings: diagonal pale carved stone mid lane, low grass and bushes, shallow blue river upper left, ancient Chinese fantasy tower upper right, crisp mobile realtime game textures. One small armored sword hero at 53% width/height, facing upper right. No UI, minimap, buttons, rings, targeting overlays, health bars, labels, text, watermark or phone frame.

## Shooter training ground

Production landscape gameplay-world background for an interactive touch-test replica of a mobile tactical battle royale. Match the ordinary third-person camera and visual appearance of Game for Peace/PUBG Mobile: rural Erangel-style training field, midday sky, short dry grass, blue/red containers, concrete buildings and distant trees. Helmeted character with tactical vest/backpack and M416 at lower center-left, normal in-game size. No HUD, compass, minimap, labels, text, health bars, weapon icons, joystick, controls, reticle, watermark or phone frame.

## Photo viewer sample

Natural photorealistic 16:9 high-resolution phone camera landscape. Peaceful Chinese mountain lake in clear morning, turquoise water, green hills, wooden lakeside pavilion at right bank, natural sunlight, detailed trees, rock and water surface. No people, logo, text, controls, UI or phone frame. Fine detail across the image supports pinch and pan inspection.
